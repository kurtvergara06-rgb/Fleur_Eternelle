Flower care

Keep them
blooming longer.

Simple care helps each flower stay fresh and graceful for longer.

✂

Trim stems diagonally

Cut one to two centimeters from each stem using clean scissors.

💧

Change water often

Refresh the vase water every two days to keep it clear.

🧪

Add floral food

Use the flower food provided and follow the recommended amount.

🍃

Remove leaves below water

Keep submerged leaves out of the vase to help prevent bacteria.

◌

Clean the vase

Wash the vase before refilling it with fresh room-temperature water.

☀

Avoid direct heat

Keep flowers away from strong sunlight, heat, and ripening fruit.
